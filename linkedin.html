Tribunus is an independent, founder-led software company. We build the open-source control plane for running AI agents and inference on your own hardware. Founded in October 2024 by Julian Torres in San Francisco, California. The product we ship in 2026 is Tessera Studio — a desktop app for running large language models locally with calibrated, audited per-tensor quantization. macOS today, Linux in progress. The C++ side (Tessera) is a fork of llama.cpp that takes calibration seriously: per-tensor policy evolved by a small genetic algorithm, schema-versioned receipts, runtime agreement with the offline reference down to F16. Native speculative decoding (DFlash / DSpark). Apple Neural Engine prefill on M-series Macs. We are four things, all the time: 1. Compile-time, not runtime — decisions are frozen before execution. 2. Local-first, federated when needed — your hardware, your data, your model. 3. Authority, not ambient access — every plugin and tool gets explicit capability grants. 4. Receipts, not logs — every mutation is cryptographically bound. Tribunus is released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPL-3.0) with commercial dual-licensing available for parties who require proprietary use. The earlier "Tribunus Desktop / Compute / Cell / Federation" branding is folded into the Tessera fork; the brand is one company, the product is one product. Founded by Julian Torres. Sole maintainer. Self-funded. 2026 Founders, Inc. applicant.
