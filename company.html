Tribunus · the company

An independent software company.

Tribunus is a founder-led software company in San Francisco, founded by Julian Torres in October 2024. We build Tessera Studio — a desktop app for running large language models on your own hardware — and we build it to be verifiable.

Founded October 2024· San Francisco, California· Founder-led· Open source
Mission

Make AI infrastructure deterministic where it matters most.

Most AI infrastructure is improvised at runtime. The model is loaded, kernels are picked, and memory is allocated — all under time pressure. When something goes wrong, the system cannot tell you what it actually did. When you want to scale, you discover that every decision was made in a different place, for a different reason.

Tribunus treats inference, agent execution, and durable state as one problem with one canonical representation. What a system did should be checkable, not just claimable.

We build for engineers who want to know what their models and agents actually did — and who want that answer in a record they can inspect.

How we build

Four rules, applied to everything.

01

Compile-time, not runtime

Decisions are frozen before execution. We do not improvise correctness under load.

02

Local-first, federated when needed

Your hardware, your data, your model. Federation is an escape valve — never the default.

03

Authority, not ambient access

Every plugin, tool, and agent receives explicit capability grants. Nothing has ambient authority over your state.

04

Verifiable by construction

Every run keeps a replayable record — what loaded, what quantized, what executed. Results can be checked, not just trusted.

The product

Tessera Studio.

A desktop app for running large language models on your own machine — macOS today, Linux in progress. It is the only product we are shipping in 2026.

Tessera Studio developer preview · macOS

A Swift desktop app on top of a C++ fork of llama.cpp — calibrated per-tensor quantization, native speculative decoding, and Apple Neural Engine prefill on M-series Macs. The product site and downloads live on the Tessera subdomain.
