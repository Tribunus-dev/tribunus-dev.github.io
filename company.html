mission

Make AI infrastructure deterministic at the seams where it matters most.

Most AI infrastructure is improvised at runtime. The model is loaded, kernels are picked, and memory is allocated — all under time pressure, with no plan beyond "make it run." When something goes wrong, the system cannot tell you what it actually did. When you want to scale, you discover that every decision was made in a different place for a different reason.

Tribunus exists to compile that layer. We treat inference, agent execution, and durable state as one problem with one canonical representation. The plan is the receipt. The receipt is the audit trail. The audit trail is the trust model.

We build for engineers who want to know what their agents did, not what they were told they did.

four pillars
01

Compile-time, not runtime

Decisions are frozen before execution. The plan is the receipt. We do not improvise correctness under load.

02

Local-first, federated when needed

Your hardware, your data, your model. Federation is the escape valve — never the default, always available.

03

Authority, not ambient access

Every plugin, tool, and agent receives explicit capability grants. Nothing has ambient authority over your state.

04

Receipts, not logs

Every mutation is cryptographically bound. Logs are what you write when nothing happened; receipts are what happened.
