the five shapes

What I am looking for, in five concrete asks.

No one needs to fit all five. Most of you reading this will be a fit for exactly one. Pick the one that is yours, and start there.

01

Beta users on macOS and Linux

Run Tessera Studio on a real model, on a real machine, with a real workload.
// shape 01

The fastest way to help is to use the product and tell me what broke. I ship to early users first, not to marketing. macOS is the live target; Linux is in active development.

If you are willing to install a developer-preview app, run a real model (Llama, Qwen, Gemma — the calibrated-quantized ones ship first), and file honest issues on the GitHub repo, you are shape 01.

→ first email: hello@tribunus.dev with subject "Shape 01 — beta user" · what to include: your machine (Mac model / Linux distro), the model you want to run, the workload (chat, batch, eval, agent)
02

Co-engineers

Swift / SwiftUI on the app side. C++ / quantization on the inference side. UX where it matters.
// shape 02

Small, focused PRs. I review personally. I do not have a large team — I have a small list of people I trust to land a clean diff, and I am always looking to add one or two more.

The areas where I can use help right now: SwiftUI on the macOS app (Tessera Studio is a Swift package), C++/CMake on the Linux build of the inference side, Core ML / ANE if you have shipped a real model to Apple's Neural Engine, and UX / macOS HIG where the surface needs polish.

→ first email: hello@tribunus.dev with subject "Shape 02 — co-engineer" · what to include: a link to a PR you are proud of (anywhere), the area you want to work in, and ~5 lines on why
03

Quantization researchers

People who have read the T640 / DFlash / DSpark papers and want to push them.
// shape 03

Tessera is a fork of llama.cpp that takes calibration seriously. Per-tensor policy is evolved by a small genetic algorithm; the receipts are schema-versioned; the runtime agrees with the offline reference down to F16.

If you have a hypothesis about per-tensor quantization, mixed-precision attention, or a new drafter model — the contribution surface is the receipt. Show me a result that survives replay, and we have something to ship together.

→ first email: hello@tribunus.dev with subject "Shape 03 — quantization research" · what to include: a link to a paper or a writeup, and one sentence on what you would push next
04

Advisors

People who have shipped a Mac desktop product or a fork of llama.cpp.
// shape 04

Specifically people who have done one of three things: (a) shipped a Mac desktop product to a paying audience, (b) maintained a serious fork of llama.cpp, or (c) been a technical co-founder of a small, focused, profitable company.

I pay in equity, attention, and prompt replies. I do not need a board seat; I need an hour a week and someone who will tell me when I am wrong. The bar is real shipping, not credentials.

→ first email: hello@tribunus.dev with subject "Shape 04 — advisor" · what to include: one product you shipped, one hard call you made, one thing you would push me on
05

Investors & accelerators

Pre-seed, evidence-led. I do not raise on hype.
// shape 05

I am applying to startup accelerators in 2026 with Tribunus. I am pre-seed and self-funded. The company has shipped artifacts (the Tessera fork and the Studio app) and has receipts (calibration, spec-decoding telemetry, an evidence trail from imatrix to GGUF).

If you back technical founders with shipped artifacts and you do not require a market-size slide to engage, the right email is below. I do not raise on hype. I raise on evidence and a plan that survives replay.

→ first email: julian@tribunus.dev with subject "Shape 05 — investor" · what to include: your fund / program, your check size, and one technical founder you have backed

What I am not looking for.

Pitch-deck-only conversations. If you want a deck, the receipts and the GitHub are the deck. I do not have a 12-slide narrative; I have a C++ fork, a Swift app, and a research surface.

"AI wrapper" advice. Tessera Studio is a desktop app for running models locally with calibrated quantization. It is not a chat wrapper over a remote API. If that distinction does not land, we are not the right fit.

Full-time hires in 2026. I am one founder. The first full-time hire will be after a pre-seed close, and the role will be on the Tessera side. I will post it publicly when the time comes — please do not cold-pitch your way in before then.
